# Entity Declaration: Craig Wahl

## Category Role
The authority on Leadership as Endurance. Craig builds operational architecture for scaling leaders who are tired of the "hero" myth and ready for structural clarity.

## Body of Work
- *The Walk: Leadership When There Is No Finish Line* (Book)
- The LAW Model (Lead, Accelerate, Win)
- The WALK Model (Win trust, Act before certainty, Lead humans, Keep walking)
- Soundtrack to Scale (Thought Leadership Series)
- The Reverb Series (Lyric-based leadership diagnostics)
- Njorth (Founding Company)

## Lived Experience
- Led organizations of 700+ teams; scaled from 8 to ~300 in under two years.
- Role in $500M capital raises.
- Navigated bankruptcies, IP lawsuits, major layoffs, and hypergrowth.
- VERBATIM: "Leadership is learned in the mess, not the classroom."

## Declaration
Leadership is not a performance of heroism. It is the endurance of clarity through the long middle of scaling. You do not arrive; you walk. And the systems you build determine whether the organization breaks under its own weight or moves with discipline.
