# Category Language Rules

## Canonical Terms
- **Endurance** — The core state; never "resilience" (which implies bouncing back) but "endurance" (which implies moving forward).
- **Long Middle** — The scaling phase where most break; never "growth stage."
- **Strategic Drift** — Misalignment disguised as motion; never "burnout."
- **The Walk** — Leadership as rhythmic, grounded movement, not a sprint to a finish line.
- **Signal** — Friction and clarity as diagnostic data; never "noise."

## What We Never Say
- "Hero leader" — Reinforces fragility.
- "Myth of arrival" — Endurance has no finish line.
- "Cosmetic change" as a solution — Ignores structural drift.
- "Hustle" — Endurance requires rhythm, not force.

## Usage
These terms are non-negotiable in all category content, licensing, and educational materials.
