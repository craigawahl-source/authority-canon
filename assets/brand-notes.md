# Brand & Asset Notes

## Visual Anchors

**Njorth:** Built for the long winter. Nordic restraint, stone and timber, structures that hold when conditions turn.

**Red as Signal:** Red marks friction, not danger. Used once per frame, always pointing at the work.

**The Walk:** Execution in motion. Steady, grounded forward movement, rooted in a rebuild that started with learning to walk again.

## Voice Standards

Pragmatic, anti-theoretical, grounded in operational reality.
Never use "hero," "hustle," or "myth of arrival" as positive frames.
Canonical phrases must be used verbatim in all assets.

## Routing

Visual system development routes to Martha (Chamber 02). All assets must align with the Entity Declaration and Voice Standards in the Authority Canon™.
