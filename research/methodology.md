# Methodology Notes

## Core Premise
Leadership is not a trait or a destination; it is a continuous walk requiring systemic clarity and endurance.

## Diagnostic Approach
- Treat misalignment and toxicity as signals of broken structures, not character flaws.
- Replace heroism with sherpa-like guidance.
- Build operational empathy into scaling architecture.

## Cross-Domain Synthesis
Fusion of operational rigor (COO background) with visceral metaphors (music, canine behavior, mythology). This synthesis creates diagnostic tools that are both structurally sound and emotionally resonant.
