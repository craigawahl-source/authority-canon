# The Permission Map

## Definition
Diagnostic showing how leader behavior grants cultural permission.

## Components
6 areas scored 1–5:
1. Decisions
2. Candor
3. Pace
4. Accountability
5. Learning
6. Succession

## Function
Identifies unwanted cultural permissions and designs rituals to change them.

## Application Context
Pre-workshop diagnostics, leadership team alignment, Day 90 retakes.

## What It Replaces
Guesswork about "culture" and unspoken behavioral norms.
