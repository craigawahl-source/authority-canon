# Framework: The WALK Model

**Definition:** The operating structure for the Leadership Accelerator Workshop. Moves leaders from friction to endurance through four deliberate phases.

**Components:**
- **W – Win trust:** Part II focus. Trust and Curiosity.
- **A – Act before certainty:** Part II focus. Accountability and Taking Action.
- **L – Lead humans, not head count:** Parts III–IV focus. Transparency, Vulnerability, and Toxicity Response.
- **K – Keep walking:** Part V and close. Succession, Endurance, and the Winston Loop.

**Application Context:** Leadership Accelerator Workshop.
**What It Replaces:** The previous reuse of the LAW Model for acceleration work.
